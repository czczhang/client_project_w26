Application Materials

Craft a resume that clearly shows your skills, impact, and growth.

This page gathers the key steps, resources, and reminders UMSI students can use when building or revising a resume for internships, jobs, and campus opportunities.

What makes a resume stronger?

A strong resume is easy to scan, tailored to the opportunity, and focused on evidence. Instead of only listing duties, aim to show what you improved, created, organized, or delivered.

  • Clarity: Use consistent formatting and plain language.
  • Relevance: Match your content to the role or industry.
  • Results: Highlight impact with action verbs and specifics.

Before you submit

  • Check that headings and sections are easy to scan.
  • Proofread for spelling, dates, punctuation, and alignment.
  • Ask whether every bullet shows a skill, action, or outcome.
  • Review the document on both laptop and mobile screens.

Resume writing steps

  1. Download a UMSI resume template or review a trusted sample.
  2. List your experiences, projects, coursework, leadership, and technical skills.
  3. Write bullet points that explain what you did and why it mattered.
  4. Tailor the wording and order of content for each opportunity.
  5. Review with a coach, classmate, or rubric before sending it out.

Bullet point checklist

  • Start with a strong action verb.
  • Name the task, tool, or context when helpful.
  • Show the result, audience, or purpose of the work.
  • Use numbers when they make your impact clearer.

Common questions

  • How long should my resume be? Most students should keep it to one page unless they have a strong reason for more space.
  • Should I customize my resume? Yes. Tailoring for each role helps employers understand your fit faster.
  • Can I include coursework? Yes, especially when projects or assignments demonstrate relevant skills.
  • How often should I update it? Update it after each semester, new experience, or major project.
