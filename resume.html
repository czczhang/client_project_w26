Resume Support

Craft a Resume that Tells Your Story

Your resume is often the first impression employers have of you. A clear and well-organized resume helps highlight your strengths, skills, and experiences.

This page provides guidance and trusted resources to help UMSI students create strong resumes and prepare application materials.

Resume Writing Steps

  1. Download a UMSI resume template.
  2. List your experiences, projects, and coursework.
  3. Write clear bullet points showing your impact.
  4. Tailor your resume to each opportunity.
  5. Review and edit carefully.

Resume FAQ

  • How long should my resume be? Most students should keep their resume to one page.
  • Should I customize my resume? Yes. Tailor it for each job or internship.
  • Can I include coursework? Yes, especially if it demonstrates relevant skills.
  • How often should I update it? Update it after each semester or new experience.
