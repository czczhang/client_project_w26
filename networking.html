Professional Relationships

Build confidence in networking by starting with small, clear actions.

Networking can feel intimidating at first, but it becomes much easier when you focus on curiosity, preparation, and respectful follow-up rather than trying to make a perfect impression.

Step 1

Identify professionals, alumni, or peers whose work aligns with your interests.

Step 2

Reach out with a short, specific introduction and a simple ask.

Step 3

Prepare thoughtful questions for a brief informational conversation.

Step 4

Send a thank-you note and keep track of what you learned next.

Why networking matters

Networking helps students learn how real roles work, discover opportunities, and build relationships that can shape later internships, mentoring, and job searches.

Sample outreach message

Hi [Name], I am a UMSI student interested in learning more about your work in [industry or role]. If you have time, I would appreciate the chance to ask a few questions about your path and experience.

Keep it brief, personalize one detail, and make the request easy to answer.

Follow-up reminder

After a conversation, send a thank-you message within a day or two and write down what you learned, including any advice, names, or next steps worth revisiting.

Informational interview questions

  • What does a typical day look like in your role?
  • What skills matter most for this field right now?
  • What helped you the most when you were getting started?
  • What advice would you give someone entering this area?

Conversation tips

  • Research the person before you reach out.
  • Ask open-ended questions rather than yes-or-no questions.
  • Respect time limits and end on schedule.
  • Focus on learning, not immediately asking for a job.
