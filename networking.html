Networking & Building Connections

Build relationships that support your career goals

Networking is not about "selling yourself." It is about learning, building trust, and staying connected. You can start small: one message, one conversation, one follow-up.

On this page, you'll find simple steps, message templates, and trusted UMSI resources to help you connect with alumni, peers, and professionals.

A simple networking plan

  1. Choose a focus: pick one area (UX, data, product, library/information work, etc.).
  2. Find 3 people: alumni, speakers, classmates, or people in roles you're curious about.
  3. Send 2 messages: short, polite, and specific.
  4. Schedule 1 conversation: 15-20 minutes is enough.
  5. Follow up: send a thank-you and write down next steps.

Informational interviews: questions you can ask

  • What does a normal week look like in your role?
  • What skills matter most for success on your team?
  • What do you wish you knew when you were applying for roles like this?
  • What kinds of projects stand out in applications?
  • Is there anyone else you recommend I talk to?

Track your progress

Keeping a simple tracker makes networking easier. Here are a few columns you can use in a notes doc or spreadsheet:

  • Name + role
  • How you found them
  • Date messaged
  • Status (waiting / scheduled / completed)
  • Key notes + next step
