Networking & Building Connections

Why Networking Matters

Networking helps students learn about careers, build relationships, and discover opportunities. Talking with professionals, alumni, and peers can provide helpful insights about industries and roles.

Simple Networking Plan

  1. Identify professionals or alumni in fields that interest you.
  2. Send a short introduction message.
  3. Schedule a brief informational conversation.
  4. Follow up with a thank-you message.

Sample Outreach Message

Hi [Name], I am a UMSI student interested in learning more about your work in [industry/role]. If you have time, I would love to ask a few questions about your career path and experiences.

Informational Interview Questions

  • What does a typical day look like in your role?
  • What skills are most important for this field?
  • What advice would you give to someone entering this career?
  • What resources helped you the most when you started?
