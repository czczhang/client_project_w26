Career Exploration

Explore Career Paths

Career exploration helps students understand different roles, industries, and career paths. It allows you to identify interests and skills that align with your goals.

Questions to Ask Yourself

  • What topics or problems interest me the most?
  • What type of work environment do I enjoy?
  • What skills do I want to develop?
  • What kind of impact do I want my work to have?

Ways to Explore Careers

  • Attend career fairs and employer events.
  • Talk with professionals in fields of interest.
  • Review job descriptions to learn common skills.
  • Participate in projects, internships, or research.

Common Career Paths for UMSI Students

  • User Experience (UX) Designer or Researcher
  • Data Analyst or Data Scientist
  • Product Manager
  • Information or Library Professional
  • Technology Consultant
