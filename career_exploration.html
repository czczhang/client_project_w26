Career Exploration

Explore paths before you decide

Career exploration is about learning what roles exist, what they involve, and how they match your interests, values, and skills. You do not need to have everything figured out right away.

This page highlights simple ways to explore options and gather information before applying for jobs or internships.

Questions to guide your exploration

  • What kind of problems do I enjoy working on?
  • Do I prefer technical, people-focused, or mixed roles?
  • What skills do I want to use or build?
  • What work environments feel right to me?

Ways to explore careers

  • Read job descriptions: notice common skills and responsibilities.
  • Talk to people: informational interviews help you learn real day-to-day work.
  • Attend events: career fairs, panels, and employer info sessions.
  • Try projects: class projects, volunteer work, or personal projects.

Common roles UMSI students explore

  • User Experience (UX) Researcher or Designer
  • Data Analyst or Data Scientist
  • Product Manager
  • Information or Library Professional
  • Consultant or Analyst

UMSI career exploration resources

    Browse jobs, internships, employer events, and career outcomes.
  • Talk to a Career Coach
    Get help identifying interests and narrowing career options.
  • Attend a Career Event
    Learn about roles and industries directly from employers and alumni.
